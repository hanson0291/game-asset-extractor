Airplane controls
=================

Fixed-wing aircraft (planes and jets, including the 007 First Light
'Drone Webb') automatically use assisted airplane flight rather than the
road-car controller. Press E / Pilot after starting Play. Fullscreen is
optional. A plain quadcopter 'drone' is not an airplane and keeps the
ground controller.

Touch:
- Left stick (STICK): pitch and roll. Push up to climb once airborne,
  pull down to descend, tilt sideways to bank and turn.
- Hold the ▲ Throttle / ▼ Throttle buttons to raise or lower power.
  Throttle stays where you set it — you do not need to hold the stick.
- Right stick (RUDDER / CAM): sideways steers on the ground (rudder)
  and trims the turn in the air; up/down tilts the chase camera.

Keyboard:
- W / S: throttle up / down (hold). The throttle level persists.
- Arrow keys: pitch (up/down) and roll (left/right).
- Q / R: rudder left / right.
- Space: wheel brakes on the ground, airbrake in the air.
- E remains the enter/exit action — land first; exit is blocked airborne.

Mouse:
- Drag: orbit the chase camera.
- The on-screen THR buttons work with the mouse exactly as with touch.

Runway fleet (McKenzie Field):
- Load McKenzie Field with no vehicle selected and every fixed-wing
  aircraft in the catalog parks in a line on the runway.
- Walk up to any parked plane and press E to fly it. Land, then press E
  again to step out beside it. Picking a vehicle from the list keeps the
  old behaviour — that one vehicle beside your character, and no fleet.

Flight model:
- Build speed on the runway at full throttle. At the rotate speed the
  HUD reads 'PULL BACK TO TAKE OFF' — pull the stick back to lift off.
- Bank to turn: banked wings turn the aircraft (a coordinated turn), and
  climbing bleeds airspeed while diving regains it.
- Slow down too much with the nose up and the aircraft stalls and sinks;
  lower the nose and add power to recover.
- The HUD shows heading, height above the launch surface (AGL, meters),
  indicated airspeed (knots), vertical speed (m/s), throttle percentage,
  elevator command, pitch and bank on an artificial horizon.
- This is assisted demo flight, not a full aerodynamic or terrain
  simulation. The demo ceiling is 150 m AGL. Land before exiting; Stop
  and Reset remain available independently of flight state.
